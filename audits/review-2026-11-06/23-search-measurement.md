# Auditor 23 — Search measurement and indexing diagnosis

6 November 2026. Reviewed historical November 3–5 audit records, current analytics/IndexNow source and today's shared HTTP evidence. No new signed-in Search Console access, export, account change or indexing submission performed.

## Findings

1. **P1 investigation — The historical impression decline remains unexplained.** The November 4 report records site impressions falling from 1,387 in August to 138 in September and 92 in October; English robotics falls from 1,143 to zero in both later months. These are previously observed figures, not fresh exports. The historical late-September/October robots failures cannot alone explain a break already visible in late August. Today's 106 successful responses establish current availability only. Obtain daily query/page evidence around the breakpoint before attributing the loss to robots, an algorithm, seasonality or a penalty. Preserve the distinction between impressions and clicks: the earlier broad robotics exposure generated zero recorded clicks.

2. **P2 measurement gap — Acquisition is not connected to a measured qualification funnel in the inspected frontend.** `src/app/layout.tsx:7,166` installs Vercel Analytics. A source search found no explicit `track`, `gtag`, `dataLayer` or comparable conversion-event calls. External/backend tracking may exist and was not inspected. First inventory existing analytics, then measure course view → successful trial request → qualified enquiry → enrolment, segmented by landing page, locale and acquisition source. Keep identifiers/contact details, child information and free-text responses out of analytics payloads. An optional self-reported discovery source should be stored separately from measured referrer attribution.

3. **P2 observability gap — IndexNow notification is not indexing confirmation.** `src/app/api/indexnow/route.ts:119–128` returns upstream status and submission count. Revalidation schedules the notification and logs only failures (`src/app/api/revalidate/route.ts:72–85`); the revalidation success response therefore does not prove notification or indexing success. Verify deployed configuration and retained logs without exposing secrets. Record attempted/accepted notifications separately from subsequent crawl/index evidence. Official [IndexNow documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation) says HTTP 200 confirms receipt, not inclusion. No submission was made by this audit.

## Concrete diagnostic dataset and sequence

Include page, query, country, device and search type; record property, report time and filters. Analyze robotics separately, look for alternate canonical/URL gains, and segment UAE commercial parent intent from broad engineering queries. Retain absolute volumes; do not interpret aggregate average position as a fixed-keyword ranking.
2. Capture current sitemap report and URL Inspection for `/en`, `/ar`, both UAE pages, robotics and Python, plus representative Arabic articles. Record indexed state, Google-selected versus declared canonical, last crawl, fetch result and report dates. Keep live test results separate from indexed-version results.
3. Correlate the first sustained loss date with late-August deployment/content/redirect history and retained edge/runtime/upstream logs. Verify crawler identity before attributing blocked requests. If historical logs are unavailable, mark root cause unresolved and establish prospective regional endpoint monitoring.
4. Review equal complete periods after verified changes; track qualified enquiries alongside impressions/clicks. Historical 44 indexed/105 not indexed counts and “Couldn't fetch” sitemap status must be rechecked before being repeated as current facts. Do not repeat old fixed code findings as open defects.

This follows Google's [traffic-drop investigation guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops): compare affected pages/queries and technical reports, while distinguishing possible causes from proven ones. No traffic recovery, live indexing count, AI traffic total or conversion loss is established here.
