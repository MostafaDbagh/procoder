# SEO and AI discovery audit — 10 November 2026

## Scope and production evidence

Audited the Next.js frontend source, all 106 URLs in the public production sitemap, and 44 discovery HTTP probes. Evidence: `seo-2026-11-10-live.json`, `discovery-2026-11-10-live.json`, and `seo-2026-11-10-local.json`.

All 106 sitemap pages returned 200 after retrying four initial timeouts. No duplicate titles or descriptions were found. All had a matching self-canonical, English/Arabic alternates including the page's own language, H1 content, and parseable JSON-LD. No missing image alt attributes or unexpected noindex directives were found in these pages. These checks establish crawl readiness, not indexing or rankings. An alt attribute's presence does not establish its descriptive quality.

43 of 44 discovery probes passed. The production short LLM index omitted the Arabic UAE guide link required by the existing monitor; that link is now added locally. Requests with declared Google, Bing, OpenAI, Claude and Perplexity user agents succeeded in this probe. This does not test verified provider IPs or prove citations.

## Prioritized remaining improvements

1. **Deploy and verify the corrected artifacts.** Repeat discovery probes after deployment; inspect representative English/Arabic course, blog and parent pages in Search Console. Confirm sitemap processing and actual index coverage. Account data was not accessed in this audit.
2. **Restore local backend availability.** The frontend compiles, but live course and stage rendering depends on the API. Check the configured backend process/connectivity before evaluating those pages locally. Keep distinguishing upstream failure from a genuine missing course.
3. **Measure actual AI visibility.** Use Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance for citations and grounding queries, Search Console for organic landing pages, and analytics for referral/conversion trends. Use stable bilingual queries and record dated observations; do not confuse declared-bot HTTP accessibility with recommendations.
4. **Strengthen evidence on course and author pages.** Add verified instructor/author biographies, real qualifications, syllabus outcomes, example student projects with permission, equipment requirements, confirmed teaching languages, prices and scheduling details where known. Surface them visibly before reflecting them in schema. Do not invent review ratings, dates, credentials or local offices.
5. **Review snippet length editorially.** Eleven production titles exceed 65 characters and eight descriptions exceed 170. These are review heuristics, not search limits or errors. Prioritize pages with poor measured CTR; retain natural Arabic wording and avoid arbitrary truncation.
6. **Measure performance before optimizing.** Collect mobile Core Web Vitals/CrUX or real-user data, then inspect hero imagery, fonts and animation/client bundles. This audit did not collect field performance data, so it does not assign a performance score.
7. **Content consistency upkeep.** Verify rescheduling/trial policies with the actual business policy, and review long-form LLM curriculum examples whenever visible learning paths change. Keep live catalog data authoritative; do not expand unsupported marketing claims in discovery text.
8. **Consider dedicated subject landing pages only with substantive content.** Current category query views consolidate to the catalog. Distinct indexable pages may be useful for robotics/Python/AI when they contain unique guidance, relevant live courses and real search demand; avoid thin duplicated pages.
